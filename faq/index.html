Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about our role and how we work.

Do you approve shop drawings or designs?

No. We review, comment and recommend; final approval rests with the client and its appointed parties. That keeps our review independent.

What standard do you design temporary works to?

BS 5975 for procedural control and design, with structural and geotechnical design to the Eurocodes and applicable local standards.

Can you check a design someone else produced?

Yes. Independent design review and proof-checking of others’ designs is a core service.

Do you provide signed calculations?

Temporary-works designs are issued with calculations and drawings signed by a competent engineer and checked at the assigned category.

Where is the work performed?

Engineering and review are performed from our Singapore office and delivered digitally; site attendance is short-term and advisory where agreed.

What is a permit-to-load?

Written authorisation that a temporary-works structure may be loaded, issued only after design, check and inspection confirm readiness.

Do you provide value engineering?

Yes — buildability and material optimisation that preserves performance, quantified and submitted for client approval.

Who owns the deliverables?

Designs, calculations and reports transfer to the client on payment, subject to the written engagement.

Where are you based?

138 Robinson Road, #24-01 Oxley Tower, Singapore 068906.

How do we start?

Contact support@veyronsg.com or use the contact form. We will arrange a short call to understand the project and propose a way forward.

Do you work internationally or remotely?

Yes. Most work can be delivered through digital collaboration with agreed deliverables and reporting cadence.

How are engagements structured and priced?

Each engagement has a written scope, deliverables and fee, typically on a man-month or deliverable basis.

Can you join a live project?

Yes. We can mobilise on projects already underway, establish a working basis quickly and refine from there.

How quickly can you mobilise?

Timing depends on scope and availability. We first establish a credible working basis and the required data interfaces.

Who will do the work?

Senior specialists. Veyron is deliberately a small, hands-on practice.

How do you assure reliable output?

Senior review, version control, recognised standards and separation between producer and reviewer where independence is required.

Who owns project data?

The client does. We protect confidential information and transfer engagement outputs on payment.

What do you need from us?

A clear point of contact, relevant project information and agreement on scope, deliverables and cadence.

Do you sign a non-disclosure agreement?

Yes. We can work under your NDA or ours and treat project information as confidential by default.

How do you report and communicate?

Through a single point of contact on a fixed cadence, with prompt escalation of cost, programme, quality or risk issues.
