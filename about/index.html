About Veyron

Independent engineering judgement for infrastructure.

We combine design-office rigour with the realities of the construction site.

Who we are

Specialists alongside owners and contractors.

Veyron Infrastructure Engineering is a Singapore-based specialist engineering and independent design-review practice.

We work on roads, drainage, utilities and structures, providing temporary-works design, independent technical review and value engineering that keep infrastructure projects safe, buildable and efficient.

Our engineers work to BS 5975, the Eurocodes and applicable local standards, supported by recognised structural and geotechnical analysis tools.

We provide review and recommendations; we do not hold the approval authority for the works we comment on. Final approval rests with the client and its appointed parties. This keeps our review genuinely independent and our advice candid.
