Our approach

Control the risk before the works are loaded.

Every temporary-works item is briefed, designed, independently checked at the right category, and released by permit before loading.

Method

Five steps from basis of design to safe striking.

The workflow is a genuine control system, not a sequence of forms.

01 · BRIEF

Brief & basis

Agree loads, ground parameters, design life and check category in writing before calculation.

02 · DESIGN

Design

Chartered engineers produce calculations and drawings, recording assumptions explicitly.

03 · CHECK

Independent check

A competent checker independent of the designer verifies the work at the assigned category.

04 · PERMIT

Permit & register

No temporary works are loaded until design, check and inspection confirm readiness.

05 · SUPPORT

Support & strike

Striking is authorised against evidence that permanent works have adequate strength.
