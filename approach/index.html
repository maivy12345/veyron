Our approach

Rigorous thinking, clearly communicated.

A useful engineering outcome begins with a precise question, reliable project information and a shared understanding of what the review or design must deliver.

How we work

Four steps from brief to response.

The level of analysis and the form of deliverables are defined by the agreed scope and project stage.

01 / DEFINE

Set the brief

Confirm the design question, responsibilities, boundaries and required output.

02 / EXAMINE

Test the basis

Study drawings, calculations, loads, assumptions and construction conditions.

03 / DEVELOP

Resolve the issue

Assess findings or develop a design response with interfaces and sequence in view.

04 / DELIVER

Communicate clearly

Issue agreed observations, recommendations, drawings or calculations.
