The basis of design is the most important page nobody reads

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Every temporary-works design rests on a basis of design — the loads assumed, the ground parameters taken, the standards applied, the design life. It is usually a page or two at the front of a calculation, and it is the page that determines whether everything after it is safe or not.

Most temporary-works failures trace back not to a maths error but to a basis-of-design error: a surcharge that was never combined, a groundwater level taken optimistically, a plant load underestimated. The calculation was correct; the assumptions were wrong.

This is why we agree and record the basis of design explicitly before any calculation begins, and why an independent check starts there rather than in the numbers. Get the basis right and the design is defensible; get it wrong and no amount of careful arithmetic saves it.

Read the first page. It is where the real engineering decisions are made.
