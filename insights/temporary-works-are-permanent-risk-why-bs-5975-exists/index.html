Temporary works

Temporary works are permanent risk: why BS 5975 exists

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Temporary works — shoring, falsework, platforms and dewatering — cause a disproportionate share of serious construction incidents.

They are designed quickly, used hard and removed, which is exactly why they deserve formal control.

The discipline is the point

BS 5975 provides that control. It defines roles — the Temporary Works Coordinator and Designer — and a procedure: a design brief, a design, an independent check at a category matched to risk, and a permit before anything is loaded.

The most dangerous temporary works are those treated as too simple to check. A trench box used at the wrong depth, falsework loaded before verification, or a working platform sized by eye are where things go wrong.

Our position

Every temporary-works item gets a brief, a design, an independent check at the right category and a permit. No exceptions, because the cost of an exception is measured in people.
