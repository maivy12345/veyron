The design life of temporary works is shorter than you think — and that is the risk

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Temporary works are designed for a short life: weeks or months, not the decades of the permanent structure. It is tempting to treat that short life as low risk. It is the opposite — the short, intense life is precisely what makes temporary works dangerous.

A short design life means high utilisation: the system is loaded near its capacity, used hard, and exposed to changing conditions — weather, surcharge, modification — over its brief life. There is little margin and little time to notice a problem developing.

It also breeds complacency: ‘it’s only temporary’ is the mindset behind many incidents. The structure does not know it is temporary; it fails just as completely.

We design temporary works for the demanding, high-utilisation life they actually lead, with inspection through that life, because ‘temporary’ describes the schedule, not the risk.
