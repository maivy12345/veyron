The pour that cannot wait: designing for continuity

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Some construction operations cannot simply be paused. A large concrete pour, once started, must continue — a cold joint in the wrong place is a defect built into the permanent works. Temporary works supporting such operations carry a special burden: they cannot fail or be adjusted mid-operation.

Designing for continuity means anticipating what the operation needs from start to finish: the falsework loaded for the full pour, the access maintained, the contingency for a plant breakdown or a delivery delay. The design has to assume the operation will run to completion, because stopping is not an option.

The failures here are unforgiving precisely because there is no pause button. A support that was marginal becomes a problem with tonnes of concrete already placed and nowhere to go.

We design temporary works for continuous operations to carry the full sequence without pause, with contingency built in, because the one thing such operations cannot do is wait.
