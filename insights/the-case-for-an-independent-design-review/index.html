Independent review

The case for an independent design review

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Designers are skilled, but they are human and close to their own work.

An independent review brings a fresh expert perspective that catches assumptions the original team can no longer see — an uncombined load case, an optimistic ground parameter or a clash between disciplines.

Find risk while it is still a redline

A risk found during review is a redline on a drawing. The same risk found during construction is a stoppage, redesign and often a claim. The earlier it is found, the cheaper it is.

More than a tick-box

An effective review challenges the basis of design, tests critical load paths and flags constructability problems. It returns clear, prioritised findings the team can act on.

Independence matters. A reviewer with no stake in defending the original design will say what needs to be said.
