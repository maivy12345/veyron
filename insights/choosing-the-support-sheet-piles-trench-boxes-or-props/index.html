Choosing the support: sheet piles, trench boxes, or props?

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Every excavation that cannot stand unsupported needs a choice of support, and the choice is not just cost — it is safety, ground compatibility and constructability. Picking the support by habit rather than by analysis is how excavations get into trouble.

Trench boxes protect workers in a trench but do not always restrain ground movement; sheet piling restrains ground and water but takes time and plant; propped walls suit deeper or more sensitive excavations. Each suits some ground and loads and not others.

The right choice flows from the ground conditions, the depth, the proximity of adjacent assets, and the sequence of work. Made well, it is invisible; made by default, it shows up as settlement, collapse or delay.

We select and design excavation support from the ground and the loads, not from habit, so the method suits the conditions it actually has to work in.
