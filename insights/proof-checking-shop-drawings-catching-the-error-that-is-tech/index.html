Proof-checking shop drawings: catching the error that is technically correct

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Shop drawings translate the design into what is actually fabricated and installed. They can be perfectly drawn and still be wrong — wrong because they followed a design assumption that does not survive contact with the real geometry, or because two trades’ drawings disagree.

Proof-checking shop drawings is not re-drawing them; it is an independent technical read for the errors that pass a casual review: a connection that cannot be made in the available space, a bar arrangement that cannot be placed, a level that conflicts with an adjacent service.

We comment and recommend — final approval rests with the client — but a rigorous review at this stage catches the buildable error while it is still a redline, not a site stoppage.

We review shop drawings for what is actually buildable, not just what is technically drawn, so the conflicts surface on paper rather than at installation.
