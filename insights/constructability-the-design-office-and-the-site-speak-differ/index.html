Constructability: the design office and the site speak different languages

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

A design can be correct and still be unbuildable. The design office optimises for structural efficiency; the site cares about access, sequence, plant and tolerance. Constructability review is the translation between the two, and skipping it is expensive.

Common constructability problems are mundane and avoidable: reinforcement too congested to place and vibrate, a pour sequence that traps formwork, a connection no one can physically reach. Each is cheap to fix on a drawing and costly to fix in the field.

An independent reviewer who has seen things built — not just designed — catches these early. The value is not in finding fault but in saving the site weeks of improvised workarounds.

We review the permanent design for buildability, not just compliance, so the team that builds it isn’t fighting the drawing.
