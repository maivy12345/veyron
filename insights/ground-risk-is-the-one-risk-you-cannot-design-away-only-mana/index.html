Ground risk is the one risk you cannot design away — only manage

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Most engineering risks can be designed out: strengthen a member, change a detail, add redundancy. Ground risk is different. The ground is the one part of the project nobody designed, only investigated — and an investigation is a set of samples, not the whole truth.

That means ground risk has to be managed rather than eliminated: interpret the site investigation honestly, reason conservatively where data is thin, monitor what the ground actually does during construction, and keep a plan for the conditions you did not expect.

The projects that get into trouble are usually the ones that treated a borehole log as certainty. The ground between the boreholes is exactly where the surprises live.

We treat ground as a managed risk throughout — conservative where data is limited, monitored during the works — so surprises are caught early and handled, not discovered the hard way.
