Check categories: matching the rigour of the check to the risk of the works

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Not every temporary-works design deserves the same level of checking — and BS 5975 is explicit about it. Check categories scale the independence and depth of the verification to the risk and complexity of the design, from a simple same-team check to a fully independent organisation.

The point is proportionality. Over-checking a trivial restraint wastes effort; under-checking a complex falsework system risks lives. Assigning the right category at the design-brief stage is one of the most consequential decisions in temporary works.

The failure we see is category by convenience — everything checked at the lowest level because it is fast. That is how high-risk designs slip through with a cursory review.

We assign the check category from the brief, based on risk, and check at that level — no shortcuts on the things that can hurt people.
