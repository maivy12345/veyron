Falsework: the temporary structure that holds up the permanent one

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Falsework — the temporary support that carries formwork and wet concrete until the permanent structure can carry itself — is one of the highest-consequence temporary works on any project. It is loaded heavily, exactly once, at the moment the permanent structure is weakest.

Falsework failures are sudden and severe because the load is real and unforgiving: tonnes of wet concrete with nowhere to go if the support gives. This is precisely why BS 5975 treats falsework with the rigour it does — design, independent check at the right category, inspection, and a permit before any pour.

The common mistakes are mundane: a foundation for the falsework that was never designed, a load case that ignored construction surcharge, striking before the permanent works had gained strength. Each is avoidable with discipline.

We design and check falsework as the critical, one-shot structure it is, and authorise striking only when the permanent works can stand on their own.
