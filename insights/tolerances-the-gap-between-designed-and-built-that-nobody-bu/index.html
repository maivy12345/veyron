Tolerances: the gap between ‘designed’ and ‘built’ that nobody budgeted for

Veyron Infrastructure EngineeringTechnical insight

Drawings show a single, exact position; construction delivers a range. Every real element is built within a tolerance, and trouble arrives when the design quietly assumed perfection that the site can never deliver.

Tolerance problems compound. A column a few millimetres off, a slab marginally out of level, a connection at the edge of its allowance — individually trivial, together they can make a detail impossible to build or a fit-up impossible to achieve.

Good engineering designs for the tolerance, not against it: it checks the critical fit-ups at the limits of the allowed range, and flags where the design leaves no room for the realities of construction.

We review designs at the tolerance limits, not just the nominal dimensions, so the works can actually be built within the range the site can achieve.
