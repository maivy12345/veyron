Engagement

Working with Veyron.

Clear scope, defined deliverables, documented governance and an accountable cadence.

How an engagement starts

We begin with a short conversation to understand the project, its stage and where the pressure is. We then agree scope, deliverables and timeline in writing.

Deliverables and cadence

Each deliverable has an owner, due date and acceptance step. A regular working cycle and periodic formal report keep progress visible.

Governance and independence

We coordinate through a single client contact and escalate cost, programme, quality or risk promptly. We remain separate from the parties whose work we review because that separation makes our output credible.

Data and intellectual property

Project outputs transfer to the client on payment. We retain our background methods and protect confidential project information.

Fees

Engagements are scoped and priced in writing, typically on a man-month or deliverable basis.
