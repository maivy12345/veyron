Privacy Policy

How Veyron collects, uses and protects personal data.

Last updated 7 October 2026

Veyron Infrastructure Engineering Pte. Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your personal data and complying with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Personal data we collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, company, email address, telephone number and message when you contact us. Limited technical data may be collected through essential storage and any analytics that is configured and accepted.

Purposes

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, provide and improve services, operate and secure the website, send requested information and comply with legal obligations.

Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We may share it with service providers that operate our hosting, email and communications under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection terms, and with authorities where required by law.

Transfer, storage and retention

Data may be processed in Singapore or other jurisdictions with appropriate safeguards. Enquiry records are retained for up to 24 months after last contact unless a relationship continues; analytics retention follows the configured provider settings.

Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, encryption in transit and secure hosting.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data and withdraw consent. Contact support@veyronsg.com.
