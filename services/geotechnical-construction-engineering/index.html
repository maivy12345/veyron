Ground and construction

Geotechnical & construction engineering.

We bring ground and construction reality into the engineering.

The challenge

The ground is the one part of a project nobody fully designed.

Decisions that ignore ground conditions or construction sequence tend to surface as problems on site.

Where data is limited, we state the uncertainty and reason conservatively rather than assuming the best.

Our approach

Interpret the evidence. Respect the uncertainty.

We interpret site investigation and observed conditions, advise on earthworks, compaction, soft-ground treatment and stability, and connect analysis to the way the works will be built.

What we do

  • Interpret ground and site-investigation data
  • Advise on earthworks and soft-ground treatment
  • Optimise mass haul and cut/fill
  • Assess bearing, settlement and slope stability
  • Support construction methodology and sequence

Deliverables

  • Ground-condition interpretation
  • Compaction and treatment guidance
  • Mass-haul and cut/fill studies
  • Bearing, settlement and stability advice
  • Construction-phase engineering input
Outcome

Engineering decisions that respect the ground and how the works are built.
