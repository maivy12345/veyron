From intent to execution

Method statements & construction support.

We help turn the design into safe, executable methods on site.

The challenge

A correct design still has to survive the construction sequence.

The gap between design intent and constructed reality is where improvised workarounds — and incidents — live.

A useful method statement defines how the work will actually be executed, inspected and controlled.

Our approach

Sequence, plant, access, hold points and safe systems of work.

We prepare or review methods for earthworks, drainage, roads and structures, support technical queries during execution, and authorise striking only when evidence confirms adequate strength.

What we do

  • Method statements for civil and structural work
  • Sequence, plant and access planning
  • Protection of existing services
  • Hold points and inspection interfaces
  • Technical-query and critical-operation support

Deliverables

  • Executable method statements
  • Sequence and plant definition
  • Service-protection measures
  • Hold-point and inspection records
  • Independent advice during construction
Outcome

A clear, safe path from design intent to constructed reality.
