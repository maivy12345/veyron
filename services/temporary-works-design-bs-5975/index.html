BS 5975

Temporary works design.

We design the temporary systems that let permanent works be built safely.

The challenge

Temporary does not mean low risk.

Shoring, falsework, dewatering and platforms cause a disproportionate share of serious incidents because they are designed quickly, used hard and removed.

Treating them as too simple to formalise is how things go wrong.

Our approach

A brief, a design, an independent check and a permit.

We design to BS 5975. The brief defines loads, ground parameters and a risk-matched check category; competent engineers produce calculations and drawings; nothing is loaded until independent check and permit confirm readiness.

Everything is tracked in a temporary-works register.

What we do

  • Trench support, sheet piling and shoring
  • Dewatering and groundwater control
  • Working platforms, falsework and formwork
  • Design briefs, calculations and drawings
  • Permit-to-load and permit-to-strike regimes

Key deliverables

  • Design briefs and assigned check categories
  • Signed calculations and drawings
  • Permit records and temporary-works register
  • Inspection and readiness confirmations
  • Construction-stage technical support
Outcome

Temporary works that are designed, checked and demonstrably safe.

For owners, developers and contractors delivering infrastructure under BS 5975, Eurocodes, applicable local standards and CIRIA guidance.
