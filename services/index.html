01 / Review

Independent engineering review

An independent review helps test whether the design basis, analytical approach and resulting details respond to the real project conditions. We agree the review scope before work begins so the team knows what will be examined and how findings will be reported.

Typical review focus

  • Design criteria, loads and key assumptions
  • Calculation methods and structural behaviour
  • Drawings, details and design interfaces
  • Construction sequence and temporary conditions
  • Technical queries requiring resolution before the next stage
The purpose and level of checking depend on the agreed appointment. Statutory checking or certification, where required, must be separately confirmed for the specific project.
02 / Design

Temporary-works design

Engineered for every stage of construction.

Temporary works make construction possible. Their design needs to address the loads, ground and structural conditions that exist at each stage, as well as the way an arrangement will be installed, used and removed.

Design considerations

  1. Construction-stage loading and stability
  2. Support, propping and access arrangements
  3. Interfaces with permanent works and adjacent assets
  4. Erection, use and removal sequence
  5. Clear drawings and calculations for the agreed scope

The exact deliverables are set with the project team once site constraints and available information are understood.

03 / Coordination

Design coordination

Technical issues are rarely isolated to one drawing. Review comments, design changes and construction methods can cross the boundary between disciplines and between temporary and permanent works.

How coordination helps

  • Clarify the technical question and its design owner
  • Identify missing information and affected interfaces
  • Compare practical options against project constraints
  • Record the decision and information needed for the next step
