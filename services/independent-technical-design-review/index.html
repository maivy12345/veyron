Independent review

Independent technical / design review.

We give the permanent design a rigorous, independent challenge before it is built.

The challenge

Critical assumptions become hard to see from inside the design.

An independent expert challenge identifies risk before it reaches the site, where it becomes expensive.

We test the governing standards, load paths, ground, water and structural interfaces, and flag constructability problems.

Our role

Review and recommend — never blur the approval boundary.

We comment and recommend; final approval rests with the client and its appointed parties. This keeps the review genuinely independent.

Findings are prioritised by severity and paired with practical recommendations.

What we review

  • Geotechnical, hydraulic and structural design documents
  • Risks, inconsistencies and constructability
  • Contractor-prepared shop drawings
  • Critical interfaces and clashes
  • Proof-checks at the assigned category

Deliverables

  • Prioritised independent review report
  • Risk assessment and review register
  • Constructability observations
  • Shop-drawing comments and recommendations
  • Documented proof-check outcomes
Outcome

Risks found on paper, where they are cheap to fix — not on site.
